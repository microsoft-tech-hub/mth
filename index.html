Who We Are

From MLSA Chapter to
Mentor Tech Hub

Mentor Tech Hub (MTH) is a student-led technical community at Quest University, dedicated to helping students learn, grow, and build real-world skills in modern technologies.

We started as the official Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors (MLSA) chapter of Quest University and have since grown into something bigger. In 2024, we rebranded as Mentor Tech Hub, reflecting our expanded mission: going beyond a single program to become a full community where every student, regardless of their background, can find guidance, opportunity, and growth in tech.

Recognised in 2024 by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) and the Microsoft Student Program as one of the Top 10 Most Influential Student Tech Clubs in Pakistan.

Through technical sessions, hands-on workshops, bootcamps, mentorship programs, collaborative projects, and learning opportunities MTH has directly impacted 8,000+ students across Pakistan. We bridge the gap between classroom education and industry-ready skills in areas like AI, cloud computing, data science, cybersecurity, software development, and more.
